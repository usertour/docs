Self-hosted deployments run the **Community Edition** or use a **Business** or **Enterprise** license.

The Community Edition is licensed under [MIT](https://github.com/usertour/usertour/blob/main/LICENSE). It is free to self-host for any purpose, including production and commercial use.

A Business or Enterprise license unlocks additional self-hosted features and support. The code behind these features ships in the open-source repository under the [Enterprise License](https://github.com/usertour/usertour/blob/main/LICENSE.enterprise) and may only be used in production with a valid license. Commercial terms are governed by our [SAAP License Agreement](https://usertour.io/legal/Usertour-SAAP-License-Agreement.pdf).

## When you need a license

You need a Business or Enterprise license if:

- you want to remove Usertour branding
- you need SSO, audit log, or two-factor authentication
- you want deployment and upgrade support with a response-time SLA

Running the Community Edition in production, whether for internal tools or customer-facing products, does not require a license.

You can [contact us](https://tally.so/r/n904o1) to discuss licensing.
